Options for ranking reference TCGA modules:

  • Mean Accuracy Decrease: Ranks modules by the average loss in prediction accuracy when the module features are permuted in the Random Forest model.
  • Mean Gini Decrease: Ranks modules by how much node purity (Gini impurity reduction) is gained by splits using the module across trees.
  • Abs. Mean Enrichment Score: Ranks modules by highest magnitude of mean enrichment score across the reference TCGA samples (prioritizing strong positive or negative regulation).
  • Mean Enrichment Score: Ranks modules by signed mean enrichment score across the reference TCGA samples (highest positive score first).
  • Enrichment Score Variance: Ranks modules by score variance across the reference TCGA samples (prioritizing modules with high sample-to-sample variability).
