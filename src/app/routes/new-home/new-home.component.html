Competing Endogenous RNA (ceRNA) Sponging

MicroRNAs (miRNAs) repress gene expression by binding to target mRNAs. When multiple RNA molecules (mRNAs, lncRNAs, or pseudogenes) share miRNA binding sites, they compete for the available miRNA pool.

An increase in sponge RNA expression sequesters miRNAs, leading to an indirect positive correlation in expression among the competing target RNAs.

Multiple Sensitivity Correlation (mscor)

SPONGE calculates mscor to quantify indirect co-expression driven by shared miRNAs while removing background noise.

Empirical null-model sampling yields exact FDR-adjusted p-values, ensuring rigorous statistical confidence across pancancer datasets.
